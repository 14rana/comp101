Plan a Visit

You do not need to climb Everest to experience the Himalayas. These tips will help you plan a safe and memorable trip.

Best seasons to go

  • October - November: clear skies and great mountain views after the monsoon
  • March - April: mild weather and blooming rhododendrons
  • December - February: very cold, but quiet trails and snowy scenery
  • June - September: monsoon season - expect rain and cloudy views

What to pack

  • Warm layers - temperatures drop fast with altitude
  • Sturdy, broken-in hiking boots
  • Water purification tablets or a filter
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses - the mountain sun is strong
  • A basic first-aid kit

Popular treks

  1. Everest Base Camp trek (Nepal) - about 12 days
  2. Annapurna Circuit (Nepal) - about 15 days
  3. Markha Valley trek (India) - about 9 days

Tip: spend a few days at moderate altitude before going higher. Altitude sickness is the most common problem for new trekkers.
