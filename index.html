Welcome to the Himalayas

The Himalayas are the tallest mountain range in the world, stretching about 2,400 kilometres across Asia. Their name comes from Sanskrit and means "abode of snow".

The range passes through five countries - Bhutan, China, India, Nepal, and Pakistan - and holds all fourteen of the world's peaks above 8,000 metres, including Mount Everest. For centuries these mountains have shaped the climate, rivers, and cultures of South Asia.

Fun fact: the Himalayas are still growing! The Indian tectonic plate keeps pushing into Asia, lifting the range about 1 centimetre every year.
