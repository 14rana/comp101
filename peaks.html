Famous Peaks

The Himalayas hold the five tallest mountains on Earth. Here they are, ordered by height.

Rank Peak Height (m) Country First Climbed
1 Mount Everest 8,848.86 Nepal / China 1953
2 Kangchenjunga 8,586 Nepal / India 1955
3 Lhotse 8,516 Nepal / China 1956
4 Makalu 8,485 Nepal / China 1955
5 Cho Oyu 8,188 Nepal / China 1954

Top 5 tallest, in order

  1. Mount Everest - 8,848.86 m
  2. Kangchenjunga - 8,586 m
  3. Lhotse - 8,516 m
  4. Makalu - 8,485 m
  5. Cho Oyu - 8,188 m

Note: K2 (8,611 m) is the world's second-tallest mountain, but it stands in the Karakoram range, not the Himalayas.
